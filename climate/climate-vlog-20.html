Renewable Energy & Decarbonization

Published on January 3, 2026

Renewable energy from sources like solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, and biomass is central to decarbonizing the global economy. These energy sources produce little to no greenhouse gas emissions during operation and are increasingly cost-competitive with fossil fuels. The rapid deployment of renewables is essential for meeting climate goals and reducing air pollution.

Decarbonization extends beyond electricity to include transportation, heating, and industrial processes. This requires electrification of these sectors combined with renewable energy, as well as the development of green hydrogen and other low-carbon fuels. The energy transition also involves modernizing grids, improving energy storage, and implementing demand-side management to ensure reliability.
