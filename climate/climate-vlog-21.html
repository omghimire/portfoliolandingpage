Climate Justice & Equity

Published on January 3, 2026

Climate justice addresses the ethical and political dimensions of climate change, recognizing that those least responsible for causing climate change often suffer its worst impacts. This includes vulnerable communities, developing nations, indigenous peoples, and future generations. Climate justice demands that climate action be fair and equitable.

Key principles of climate justice include common but differentiated responsibilities, recognizing that developed countries have historically contributed more to emissions. It also emphasizes the need for climate finance to support developing countries in their mitigation and adaptation efforts. Ensuring a just transition means that the shift to a low-carbon economy must not leave workers or communities behind.
