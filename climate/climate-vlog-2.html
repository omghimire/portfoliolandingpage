Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) Concentration Trends

Published on January 3, 2026

Carbon dioxide levels in the atmosphere have been rising steadily since the Industrial Revolution. Current CO₂ concentrations are at their highest levels in at least 800,000 years, with measurements showing over 420 parts per million (ppm) as of recent years. This unprecedented increase is directly linked to human activities, particularly fossil fuel combustion and deforestation.

The Mauna Loa Observatory in Hawaii has been tracking atmospheric CO₂ since 1958, providing the longest continuous record of atmospheric CO₂ concentrations. The data shows a clear upward trend with seasonal fluctuations, demonstrating the ongoing impact of human emissions on the global carbon cycle.
