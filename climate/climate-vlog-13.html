Extreme Weather Events (Heatwaves, Storms, Floods)

Published on January 3, 2026

Climate change is increasing the frequency and intensity of extreme weather events. Heatwaves are becoming more frequent and severe, storms are intensifying, and heavy rainfall events are leading to more frequent flooding. These changes pose significant risks to human health, infrastructure, and ecosystems.

Scientific studies have shown that climate change has made certain extreme weather events more likely and more severe. For example, the probability of record-breaking heatwaves has increased significantly due to global warming. Adaptation strategies include improving early warning systems, building resilient infrastructure, and developing emergency response plans to protect vulnerable populations.
