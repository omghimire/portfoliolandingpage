Methane (CH₄) and Its Climate Impact

Published on January 3, 2026

Methane is a potent greenhouse gas with a global warming potential more than 80 times greater than carbon dioxide over a 20-year period. Although it has a shorter atmospheric lifetime than CO₂, its immediate warming impact makes it a critical target for climate mitigation efforts. Major sources of methane include agriculture (especially livestock), fossil fuel extraction, waste management, and natural wetlands.

Reducing methane emissions offers one of the most effective near-term climate solutions. Because methane breaks down in the atmosphere within decades, cutting emissions now can quickly slow the rate of warming. This makes methane reduction a key component of international climate agreements and national climate strategies.
