Global Temperature Rise & Historical Records

Published on January 3, 2026

Global temperatures have risen significantly since the late 19th century. The past decade has been the warmest on record, with each successive decade since the 1970s being warmer than the previous one. According to NASA and NOAA, global temperature has increased by approximately 1.1°C since pre-industrial times, with most of this warming occurring in the past 40 years.

Historical temperature records are reconstructed using various methods including ice cores, tree rings, coral records, and instrumental measurements. These records provide crucial evidence of the unprecedented rate of current warming compared to natural climate variations over the past millennia. The data clearly shows that the current warming trend is exceptional and cannot be explained by natural causes alone.
