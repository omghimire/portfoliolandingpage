Greenhouse Gas Emissions & Sources

Published on January 3, 2026

Greenhouse gas emissions are the primary driver of climate change. These gases, including carbon dioxide, methane, nitrous oxide, and fluorinated gases, trap heat in the Earth's atmosphere and contribute to global warming. Understanding the sources of these emissions is crucial for developing effective mitigation strategies.

The largest sources of greenhouse gas emissions include electricity and heat production, agriculture, forestry, and other land use, industry, transportation, and buildings. Each sector contributes significantly to the overall emissions profile, with fossil fuel combustion being the dominant source across multiple sectors.
