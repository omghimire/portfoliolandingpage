Climate Adaptation & Resilience Planning

Published on January 3, 2026

Climate adaptation involves adjusting to current or expected climate change and its effects. While mitigation aims to reduce the causes of climate change, adaptation focuses on managing the impacts that are already occurring or unavoidable. Building resilience means enhancing the ability of communities, ecosystems, and economies to withstand and recover from climate-related shocks.

Adaptation strategies vary by context but include building flood defenses, developing drought-resistant crops, improving water management, creating early warning systems, and designing climate-resilient infrastructure. Effective adaptation requires understanding local climate risks, engaging stakeholders, and ensuring that adaptation measures are equitable and don't increase vulnerability elsewhere.
