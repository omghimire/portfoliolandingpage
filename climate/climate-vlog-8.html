Carbon Footprint & Personal Impact

Published on January 3, 2026

A carbon footprint is the total greenhouse gas emissions caused by an individual, event, organization, service, or product, expressed as carbon dioxide equivalent. Understanding and reducing our personal carbon footprints is essential for collective climate action. Individual actions, while small on their own, can create significant impact when adopted by millions of people.

Common sources of personal carbon emissions include transportation, home energy use, diet, and consumption patterns. Simple actions like using public transport, reducing energy consumption, eating more plant-based foods, and reducing waste can significantly reduce individual carbon footprints. Calculating your carbon footprint is the first step toward understanding your impact and identifying reduction opportunities.
