Fossil Fuels & the Energy Transition

Published on January 3, 2026

Fossil fuels—coal, oil, and natural gas—have been the primary energy sources driving industrial development but are also the largest source of greenhouse gas emissions. The energy transition refers to the shift from fossil fuels to renewable energy sources and improved energy efficiency. This transition is essential for meeting climate goals and limiting global warming.

The transition involves scaling up renewable energy technologies like solar, wind, hydro, and geothermal power, while phasing out coal-fired power plants and reducing oil and gas consumption. It also includes investments in energy storage, grid modernization, and energy efficiency measures. While challenging, this transition presents significant economic opportunities and can create millions of new jobs in clean energy sectors.
