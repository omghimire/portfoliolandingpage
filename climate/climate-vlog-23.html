Agriculture & Food Security Under Climate Change

Published on January 3, 2026

Climate change poses significant challenges to agriculture and global food security. Rising temperatures, changing precipitation patterns, and increased frequency of extreme weather events affect crop yields, livestock productivity, and fisheries. These impacts threaten the ability to feed a growing global population, particularly in vulnerable regions.

Adaptation strategies in agriculture include developing climate-resilient crop varieties, improving water management, adopting sustainable farming practices, and diversifying food systems. Climate-smart agriculture aims to increase productivity, enhance resilience, and reduce emissions from the agricultural sector. International cooperation is essential to ensure food security in a changing climate.
