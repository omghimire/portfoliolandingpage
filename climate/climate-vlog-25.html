Net Zero Targets & Corporate Commitments

Published on January 3, 2026

Net zero refers to achieving a balance between the amount of greenhouse gas emissions produced and the amount removed from the atmosphere. Many countries, cities, and companies have pledged to achieve net zero emissions by mid-century. These commitments are essential for limiting global warming to 1.5°C and avoiding the worst impacts of climate change.

Corporate climate commitments have grown significantly in recent years, with major companies setting science-based targets and joining initiatives like the Race to Zero. However, the credibility of these commitments depends on having clear plans, near-term targets, and transparent reporting. Greenwashing remains a concern, making third-party verification and accountability mechanisms crucial.
