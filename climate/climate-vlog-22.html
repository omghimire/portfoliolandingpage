Climate Modeling & Future Predictions

Published on January 3, 2026

Climate models are sophisticated computer simulations that represent the Earth's climate system. These models incorporate our understanding of atmospheric physics, ocean dynamics, land surface processes, and the carbon cycle. They are essential tools for projecting future climate changes under different emission scenarios and informing policy decisions.

While climate models have uncertainties, particularly at regional scales, they have successfully predicted many observed trends over recent decades. Models are continuously improved with better data and understanding of climate processes. The latest generation of models provides increasingly detailed projections of temperature, precipitation, and extreme events, helping societies plan for different climate futures.
