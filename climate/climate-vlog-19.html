Carbon Capture, Utilization & Storage (CCUS)

Published on January 3, 2026

Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage (CCUS) refers to technologies that capture CO₂ from industrial processes or directly from the air, then either use it for commercial applications or store it permanently underground. CCUS is considered essential for achieving net-zero emissions, particularly in hard-to-abate sectors like cement, steel, and chemical production.

While CCUS technology has been demonstrated at commercial scale, its deployment needs to accelerate significantly to meet climate goals. Challenges include high costs, energy requirements, and the need for appropriate storage sites and regulatory frameworks. However, CCUS also offers opportunities for creating new industries and jobs in carbon management.
