Cloud and Cybersecurity

क्लाउड र साइबर सुरक्षा। Shared computers, and the care that keeps them trustworthy.

Cloud services

Cloud computing rents storage and processing instead of keeping every machine in the office. The useful questions are what is stored, who can reach it, and what happens if the service is down.

Identity and access

An account is a key. Unique passwords, a second check at sign-in, and access limited to the job someone actually does reduce the damage of one stolen login.

Networks

A network is the path between devices. Knowing which services face the public internet, and which stay inside an office, is the start of keeping that path narrow.

Safe habits

Most losses begin with a rushed click or a reused password. Updates, caution with unexpected messages, and a habit of checking the sender protect ordinary accounts without special equipment.

Backups and recovery

A backup is a copy you can restore, kept apart from the original. Recovery is the practice of proving that copy still opens before the day it is needed.
