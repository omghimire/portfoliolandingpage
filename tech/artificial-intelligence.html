Artificial Intelligence

कृत्रिम बुद्धिमत्ता। Systems that find patterns, and the judgment that stays with people.

What systems do

These systems recognize, sort, predict, or draft from examples they were given. They do not know a fact because it is true; they produce what fits the pattern in their training.

Machine learning

Machine learning adjusts a model until its answers match known examples. The quality of those examples, and the question the model was built to answer, decide whether the result is useful.

Everyday tools

Search, translation, captions, and writing assistants are already ordinary tools. They save time on a first draft and still need a person to check names, numbers, and claims.

Data and bias

A model repeats the gaps in its data. If a group was missing, mislabeled, or treated unfairly in the examples, the output can carry that skew into a decision.

Responsible use

A person remains accountable for a decision that affects someone else. Disclose when a draft was machine-made, keep private records out of public tools, and do not treat a fluent answer as a source.
