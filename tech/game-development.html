Game Development

खेल विकास। Rules, scenes, and controls that turn a screen into a game someone can play.

Concept

A short statement of who the player is, what they are trying to do, and what makes one attempt different from the next.

Scenes

The places the player sees: a level, a menu, or a pause screen. Each scene has a way in and a way on.

Controls

Keys, a controller, or a touch screen move the player. The same action should do the same thing every time.

Rules

Score, time, lives, and win or lose conditions. The player should be able to tell why a run ended.

Build

Art, sound, and code brought together into a version someone else can start, play, and finish.
